The changes

What we worked through together.

More room to gather

The living and dining area felt tight. The family decided they’d get more use from a larger shared space, so we opened the adjoining bedroom into the living room.

A room for laundry

The washer was in the main bathroom. We used the guest toilet and borrowed a strip of the main bathroom to create a dedicated laundry room.

Storage that earns its space

In the original primary suite, you looked straight at the beds when you opened the door, and the walk-in closet used valuable floor area for circulation. We moved the beds and planned built-in closets with more usable storage.

A larger ensuite

The original ensuite was very tight. Reworking the adjoining layout gave us room to make the bathroom larger.
