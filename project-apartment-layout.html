Expanded living space.

The family wanted an expansive living area for hosting, a proper laundry room, and smarter storage in the primary suite. Starting from the developer’s blueprint, we traded underused square footage for the spaces they would live in every day. They also intend to age in place, so clearances were weighed throughout and access was eased wherever the layout allowed.

The plans

A bedroom given back to the living room.

The original layout wedged a bedroom between the living area and the rest of the apartment. By removing its walls to create one generous gathering space, we were able to redraw the bathrooms and primary suite around how the family actually lives.

The changes

The decisions, one by one.

More room to gather

The original living and dining area felt tight. Opening the adjoining bedroom into it created one continuous, light-filled space for hosting.

A room for laundry

Originally, the washer sat inside the main bathroom. By borrowing a narrow strip from that bath, we converted the guest toilet into a dedicated laundry room.

Storage that earns its space

In the original primary suite, the door opened straight onto the beds and a walk-in closet wasted floor area on circulation. Rotating the beds made room for wall-to-wall built-ins that hold far more.

A larger en-suite

The developer’s en-suite felt cramped. Reconfiguring the surrounding closets freed up space for a noticeably larger bathroom.

Planned to age in place

The family means to stay for the long term, so every clearance was weighed with that in mind and access was adapted wherever the plan allowed. Above all, the route from the primary bedroom into its en-suite is open, direct and comfortable to use for years to come.

Custom closet

A compact bedroom, with room to sleep and to store.

In the developer’s plan, this was the primary bedroom. We carved a strip off the top of the room and gave it to the new primary bedroom as its closet.

Shifting the doorway toward the hall freed the entire back wall for a full-length built-in closet—leaving comfortable clearance for two beds without sacrificing storage.
