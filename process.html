How we work, step by step.

We work with buyers purchasing apartments on paper from Israeli contractors. This is what working with us looks like, from your first email to the day you get the keys.

Send us your plan See packages

Five steps, from plans to pillows.

  1. 01

    Send us what you have

    Whatever you have is enough to start. We’ll tell you if we need anything else.

    • The sales plan, or the latest plan you received
    • The developer’s list of standard finishes, if you have it
    • Any deadline for submitting changes
    • What you want to work better: seating for Shabbos meals, kitchen storage, a bathroom layout
  2. 02

    We review the plan with you

    In a consultation, we go through the contractor’s sales plan with you in English: real dimensions, furniture clearances, circulation and storage, and the changes worth asking for. The consultation is free with any package.

  3. 03

    You choose where you want us

    Bring us in for one stage or for the whole process: floor plan redesign, tile and fixture shopping with the kitchen design, contractor liaison, and furnishing. You can start while you’re reviewing the sales plan, or after you’ve signed while the contractor can still make changes.

    See packages and prices →
  4. 04

    We work to the contractor’s deadlines

    We time each decision to the contractor’s dates, so layout changes, plumbing and electrical points are settled before the walls go in, and finishes are chosen before they’re ordered.

  5. 05

    You follow it from abroad, in English

    You don’t need to be in Israel. We explain every decision in English, speak with the contractor and suppliers in Hebrew for you, check revised drawings against what you approved, and keep you updated. Tile selections can happen in person or remotely.

What we plan around.

Your things, coming from a U.S. home

In the U.S., you might have a coat closet, a walk-in pantry, or a basement or garage. In an Israeli apartment, coats, bulk groceries, luggage and seasonal items may all need a place inside your living space.

We look at what you use every day and where it makes sense to keep it. Sometimes shifting a doorway gives us a whole wall for storage while keeping the room easy to move through.

What you’re buying the apartment for

Living there

Moving in at handover

Plan the kitchen, storage, lighting and room layouts around how your family lives today, so the apartment is ready for daily life when you get the keys.

Later

A home for later

Use the construction stage for decisions that are harder to revisit, like walls, plumbing and electrical points. Keep the layout flexible as your plans take shape.

Rental

Buying as an investment

Weigh the contractor’s standard options against paid upgrades. Focus on a practical layout, durable finishes, and features that are easy to maintain.

Two more questions.

Do I have to use the contractor’s kitchen supplier?

No. We can work with the contractor’s kitchen supplier or design the kitchen independently.

Can you furnish the whole apartment?

Yes. We plan and source furniture room by room, design custom cabinetry, and finish each room with lighting, rugs, window treatments, art and accessories.
