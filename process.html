How we work

From sales plan to building plans.

We work with buyers purchasing apartments on paper from Israeli contractors. You can start while you’re reviewing a sales plan, or after you’ve signed while the contractor can still make changes.

Send us the sales plan and any deadlines you’ve been given, and we’ll review them together in English.

Downsizing from the U.S.

Where will everything go?

In the U.S., you might have a coat closet, a walk-in pantry, or a basement or garage. In an Israeli apartment, coats, bulk groceries, luggage and seasonal items may all need a place inside your living space.

We look at what you use every day and where it makes sense to keep it. Sometimes shifting a doorway gives us a whole wall for storage while keeping the room easy to move through.

Questions

Before you reach out.

Can I bring you in partway through?

Yes. You can bring us in when you first get the prospectus, for one specific stage, or for the entire process, from plans to pillows.

Do I need to be in Israel?

No. We’re on the ground for you and explain every decision in English. Tile selections can happen in person or remotely.

Will you speak to the contractor for me?

Yes. We prepare layout changes and discuss them directly with the contractor’s team, and we follow up with suppliers on your behalf.

Do I have to use the contractor’s kitchen supplier?

No. We can work with the contractor’s kitchen supplier or design the kitchen independently.

Can you furnish the whole apartment?

Yes. We plan and source furniture room by room, design custom cabinetry, and finish each room with lighting, rugs, window treatments, art and accessories.
