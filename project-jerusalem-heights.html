← Home Kaduri Apartments, Jerusalem

Two bedrooms became three.

No extension and no change to the outside of the building. The third bedroom was found within the apartment’s own walls.

The brief

A family living abroad wanted a home for their visits to Israel that could be rented out between stays. A third bedroom made it a stronger rental, and a more comfortable home when they are there.

The plans

Relocating the kitchen to gain a bedroom.

The developer’s plan gave the kitchen a room of its own, closed off from the living area. We moved the kitchen out into the open plan, where the laundry closet and part of the entry had been, and its original location became the third bedroom.

Custom closet

Room for the fridge, and still a full closet.

The new kitchen needed a fridge and a pantry, and the only place for them was the back of the bedroom’s closet recess. We gave them that depth, shifted the bedroom door, and designed the closet around what remained.

From the bedroom it reads as a single flush wall of closet. Inside, it works at two depths: full hanging depth within the recess, and shelving for folded clothes where the wall steps forward.

Materials

One open space, one palette.

Quartz, brushed steel and warm wood in the kitchen; linen, oak, terracotta and a faded rug in the living and dining room. The two share one open space, so they share one set of materials.
