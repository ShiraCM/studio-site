Two bedrooms became three.

The original plan had two bedrooms and a separate kitchen. We turned the kitchen into a third bedroom, then carved a compact kitchen out of the former laundry area and part of the entrance to the living room. The family gained a room to sleep in, and the living and dining area stayed open.

The brief

The family wanted a base for when they visit Israel, and planned to rent the apartment out in between. Turning two bedrooms into three let them raise the rent considerably.
