01 · Before you commit

It starts as lines on a page.

A contractor’s sales plan makes every room look generous. We read it with you in English, check the real dimensions, and draw your own furniture in, so you know how the apartment will live before you sign.

02 · Layout and selections

We build it at real size.

Layout changes, the kitchen, bathrooms, storage, tiles and fixtures, all worked out at the apartment’s actual measurements. We take your changes to the contractor in Hebrew and check every revised drawing against what you approved.

03 · Ready to move in

Then you see your home, finished.

You see each room before anything is ordered. Then we furnish and style the apartment, with built-ins made by our own tradespeople, so it’s ready to live in when you get the keys.

02 Once you’ve chosen the apartment

Make the layout work for you.

We rethink the rooms around your family: room for another bedroom, a more open living area, more built-in storage. We draw the changes and take them to the contractor’s team.

Every contractor limits what can change. We know where the room to move is, and use it.

03 When selections are due

Choose what goes into your home.

Tiles, the kitchen, air conditioning, doors and fixtures. We work with the contractor’s kitchen supplier or design the kitchen ourselves, and help you pick tiles in person or remotely.

04 Getting ready to move in

Furnish and style your home.

We plan and source furniture room by room, and design built-ins where they use the space better: a vanity fitted to the bathroom, storage set into a niche, a piece sized to keep a walkway clear. Then curtains, rugs, lighting, art and the last details.
