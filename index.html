Stage 01 תוכנית מכר “sales plan” When you get the prospectus

Is this the right apartment for you?

Before you commit, we review the contractor’s sales plan with you. We check actual dimensions, furniture clearances, circulation and storage so you can decide whether the space suits the way you live.

Checking the plan early is the cheapest moment to find a problem. After you sign, the options narrow.

Stage 02 שינויי דיירים “buyer changes” Once you’ve chosen your apartment

Make the layout work for you.

We rethink the rooms around the way you live. That might mean finding space for another bedroom, opening up the living area, or building in more storage. We prepare the changes and discuss them directly with the contractor’s team.

Every contractor sets limits on what can change. Our expertise is knowing where there’s flexibility, and using it to fit as many details as possible to your life.

Stage 03 מפרט טכני “technical specification” When it’s time to make selections

Choose what goes into your home.

We help you choose tiles, plan the kitchen, and work through choices like air conditioning, doors and fixtures.

We can work with the contractor’s kitchen supplier or design the kitchen independently, and help with tile selections in person or remotely.

See two bathrooms from plan to rendering →
Stage 04 מסירת דירה “handover” Getting ready to move in

Furnish and style your home.

We can get the entire apartment ready to live in. We plan and source furniture room by room, and design custom cabinetry where a built-in makes better use of the space: a vanity fitted to the bathroom, storage built into a niche, a piece sized to keep a walkway clear.

We finish the rooms with window treatments, rugs, lighting, art and accessories, working with our own network of local tradespeople and suppliers.

Questions

Before you reach out.

Can I bring you in partway through?

Yes. You can bring us in when you first get the prospectus, for one specific stage, or for the entire process, from plan to move-in.

Do I need to be in Israel?

No. We’re on the ground for you and explain every decision in English. Tile selections can happen in person or remotely.

Will you speak to the contractor for me?

Yes. We prepare layout changes and discuss them directly with the contractor’s team, and we follow up with suppliers on your behalf.

Do I have to use the contractor’s kitchen supplier?

No. We can work with the contractor’s kitchen supplier or design the kitchen independently.

Can you furnish the whole apartment?

Yes. We plan and source furniture room by room, design custom cabinetry, and finish each room with lighting, rugs, window treatments, art and accessories.
