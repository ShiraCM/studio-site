Meet Shira Svei

Architectural planner and interior designer

I’m an American architectural planner and interior designer. I’ve lived in Israel for 25 years and have worked in residential design for more than 15.

I know how Americans live, and what changes when you move into an Israeli apartment. My strength is making the most of the footprint, especially for clients used to more space in the States. I look for the changes that make a real difference: moving a doorway to fit a full wall of storage, or rethinking a kitchen so there’s room to host comfortably.

I’ve worked extensively with Israel’s large contractors. I know how exciting it is to buy an apartment here, and how overwhelming it can feel to make so many decisions from abroad.

I lead every project personally, with a team behind the scenes keeping the details organized and the work moving. My goal is to make the process smooth, so you can enjoy watching your plans come together.
