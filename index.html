How we help

Four stages, one team on the ground.

Buying pre-construction happens in stages. Bring us in at any one of them, or have us with you through all four.

01 When you get the prospectus

Is this the right apartment for you?

Before you commit, we review the contractor’s sales plan with you. We check real dimensions, furniture clearances, circulation and storage, so you know whether the space fits the way you live.

See how we checked one living room →
02 Once you’ve chosen the apartment

Make the layout work for you.

We rethink the rooms around your family: room for another bedroom, a more open living area, more built-in storage. We draw the changes and take them to the contractor’s team. Every contractor limits what can change; we know where the room to move is, and use it.

See an apartment that went from two bedrooms to three →
03 When selections are due

Choose what goes into your home.

Tiles, the kitchen, air conditioning, doors and fixtures. We work with the contractor’s kitchen supplier or design the kitchen ourselves, and help you pick tiles in person or remotely.

See two bathrooms from plan to rendering →
04 Getting ready to move in

Furnish and style your home.

We plan and source furniture room by room, and design built-ins where they use the space better. Then curtains, rugs, lighting, art and the last details.
